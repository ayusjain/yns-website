## About This Episode

Narendra has worked under Ayush for over a decade. In this conversation, he sits down for a leadership masterclass built not on frameworks or books, but on what leadership actually is, where it comes from, and what it costs.

From growing up in Kalahandi, one of the poorest districts in India, and studying by lantern, to leading teams of 800 people across five countries, Narendra's story is a lesson in resilience, responsibility, and purpose.

## What We Talk About

- What leadership looks like beyond titles and management frameworks
- Growing up in Kalahandi and finding a path forward through education
- The difference between taking people where they want to go and where they should go
- The role of the Gita, difficult questions, and disciplined choices
- The responsibility and cost of leading teams across countries

## Notes

A story about the kind of leader who does not simply give people the answer, but helps them find the direction they need.

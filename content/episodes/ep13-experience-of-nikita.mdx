## About This Episode

This is the second episode of The Experience Of — a shorter, sharper format built around one real experience at a time.

Nikita takes us into two wild places: a monsoon night safari in Goa, where the jungle comes alive after dark, and the Maasai Mara, where eleven lions silently plan a hunt. No beaches, no parties, no spectacle for its own sake — just the strange, patient rhythm of being fully present in the wild.

## What We Talk About

- The Western Ghats of Goa in the monsoon
- Glow-worms, scorpions, owls, flying frogs, and an Atlas moth
- Watching eleven lions plan a kill without a single roar
- Why the jungle keeps changing, and why being there means living in the moment

## Notes

Two experiences. One feeling: the world is bigger, wilder, and more alive when we pay attention.

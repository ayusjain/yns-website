## About This Episode

What does your Diwali smell like? For many of us, it is not one fragrance but a kitchen, a familiar dish, a freshly cleaned home, or a cracker going off a little too early.

Ayush sits down with Divya, Vipul, and Rama to remember Diwali across four different homes: what it smelled, sounded, and felt like, and what remains of it now. No backstory. No lesson. Just Diwali, as they remember it.

## About This Episode

This story is real. A house locked from inside for four days. Money disappearing every day with nobody home to take it. Something calling the children in their mother's voice while their mother sat right there in the room.

A call came at 4 in the morning: "Riya. Come soon." Then the CCTV caught the bottles being lifted and dropped, and the hall in complete disarray. Locked from inside. Nobody came in. Nobody went out.

## Notes

An unsettling experience, and a reminder to be aware, draw the line, and decide what you will not acknowledge.

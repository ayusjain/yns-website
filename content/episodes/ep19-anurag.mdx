## About This Episode

Anurag grew up in a village in Bihar and has been a musician all his life. At five, he sat at a harmonium during a village qawwali. Later, without a guitar of his own, he learned at a neighbor's house.

He dreamed of attending a music school, but the fees put it out of reach. In this conversation, Anurag shares what he built after that door closed: from teaching music in societies to creating a school and platform. He also talks about bands, learning by doing, and the importance of starting.

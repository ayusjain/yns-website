## About This Episode

She walked in with a mask on. Not a metaphor. An actual mask.

Because the woman behind it had published seven books, and the world that read them had never seen her face, never known her name, and never known what she did from 9 to 6.

Rheaa started writing to find answers. She kept writing even when she had none. In a quiet, brave kind of way, she built a life around words before she was ready to be fully seen.

This is the story of a writer who lived in public through her work while staying hidden behind a face that no one had ever met. And yet, somehow, the pain in her words felt so personal, so deep, so alive.

## What We Talk About

- Why she chose to stay hidden while her words reached the world
- The quiet courage of writing before you're ready to be known
- What it feels like when people know your work but not your face
- The difference between pain, expression, and the life you build around both
- The turning points that pushed her from private writing to public life

## Notes

This is a story about the quiet power of writing through pain, and the strange, beautiful fact that sometimes the deepest stories are the ones written by people who never wanted to be seen.
